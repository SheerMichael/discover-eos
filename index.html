The Creator

Rodney I. Ebrole

Ebrole is an inventor, writer, researcher, and associate professor at Western Mindanao State University in Zamboanga City.

Beyond the board, his work explores the languages and stories of the region. He writes in Filipino and Chabacano and has published research on Zamboanga’s Chabacano folklore.

History

From E-One Seven to EOS.

The game’s public record begins in Zamboanga City, where Ebrole shared an early version with the university community.

2015

Early play in Zamboanga

Western Mindanao State University profiled E-One Seven Battle of Tactics ahead of its planned appearance at the University Palaro. The original E-One Seven also appears in the 2015 Philippine copyright register.

IPOPHL copyright record
2017

Selected for iDEYA

MSU–IIT named E-One Seven among the nine projects in the first batch of its iDEYA startup incubator.

MSU–IIT announcement
2026

EOS 2.0 enters the copyright register

The Intellectual Property Office of the Philippines lists EOS 2.0 Juego de Tactica under Rodney Insertado Ebrole.
